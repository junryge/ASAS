# Brand Guidelines Skill

Create comprehensive brand guideline documents covering visual identity, voice, and usage rules.

## Document Structure

1. **Brand Overview** - Mission, vision, values
2. **Logo Usage** - Primary/secondary logos, clear space, minimum size, misuse examples
3. **Color Palette** - Primary, secondary, accent colors with hex/RGB/CMYK values
4. **Typography** - Primary and secondary fonts, hierarchy, sizing
5. **Imagery** - Photography style, illustration guidelines
6. **Voice & Tone** - Brand personality, writing guidelines
7. **Applications** - Business cards, letterhead, social media templates

## Output Format
- Generate as a structured document (docx, pdf, or markdown)
- Include color swatches with exact color codes
- Provide do's and don'ts examples
- Include spacing and sizing specifications

## Guidelines
- Ensure consistency across all brand elements
- Provide specific measurements (not vague descriptions)
- Include accessibility considerations (contrast ratios)
- Cover both digital and print applications
