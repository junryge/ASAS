# Internal Communications Skill

Draft professional internal communications for organizations.

## Communication Types

### Email Templates
- Team updates and status reports
- Project announcements
- Policy changes
- Event invitations
- Onboarding messages

### Announcements
- Company-wide announcements
- Department updates
- Product launches (internal)
- Organizational changes

### Memos
- Meeting summaries
- Decision records
- Process changes

## Structure
1. **Subject line** - Clear, actionable, concise
2. **Opening** - Context and purpose
3. **Body** - Key information, organized logically
4. **Action items** - Clear next steps with owners and deadlines
5. **Closing** - Summary or call to action

## Guidelines
- Professional but approachable tone
- Keep it concise - respect reader's time
- Lead with the most important information
- Include clear action items
- Consider the audience's perspective
- Avoid jargon unless appropriate for the audience
